# Image slots

Drop photos in `public/assets/images/` (served as `assets/images/…`) and set `data-photo` on the slot in `index.html`.

| Slot (`data-slot`) | Where | Aspect ratio | Recommended size | Notes |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| `hero-drawing` | Hero, right | 5:4 | 1500 × 1200 px | Kids drawing at a table, or a close-up of a real drawing. A star sticker sits top-right and a note bottom-left. |

Hooks on the `<figure>`:
- `--fig-duo`: `0` natural colour (recommended for this bright brand), `1` duotone.
- `--fig-shadow` / `--fig-highlight`: duotone colours.

Please only use photos of children with their guardians' written consent.
