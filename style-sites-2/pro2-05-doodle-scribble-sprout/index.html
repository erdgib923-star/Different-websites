How a class goes

From squiggle to show-and-tell.

Gold star! WOW
  1. 1

    Warm-up squiggles

    Five minutes of loops, zig-zags and spirals, eyes closed for the brave. It loosens hands and silences the "I can't draw" voice.

  2. 2

    Shapes become things

    A circle becomes a sun, a box becomes a pot. Kids learn that every drawing is built from shapes they already know.

  3. 3

    Add a story

    What grows in the pot? Who lives under the leaf? We add details by asking questions, not by correcting lines.

  4. 4

    Show and tell

    Everyone pins up their page and says one thing they like about a friend's. Then it goes home in the sketchbook.

For grown-ups

Good to know before day one.

What should they bring?
Just clothes that can get a little inky. Every marker, crayon and sketchbook is included.
How big are classes?
Eight children at most, with one teacher and one helper in every class.
Can I stay and watch?
For Tiny Scribblers, yes, there are grown-up chairs at the back. Older groups tend to draw more freely on their own.
What if they miss a week?
Drop in to Big Draw Saturday that weekend instead, free of charge.
