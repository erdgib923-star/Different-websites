How it works

From one seed to a whole floor.

seed #2047 Seamless ✓
  1. 1

    Scatter the chips

    Stones are dropped one by one and kept only if they don't touch a neighbour, even across the tile's edge.

  2. 2

    Draw the squiggles

    Wavy strokes land on top at random angles. They're allowed to overlap; that's the Memphis bit.

  3. 3

    Throw confetti

    Dots, rings and tiny triangles fill the gaps so the eye never rests too long.

  4. 4

    Tile it forever

    Anything that crosses an edge is mirrored on the opposite side, so the tile repeats with no seams.
