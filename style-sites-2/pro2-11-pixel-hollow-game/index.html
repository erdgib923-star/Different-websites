Pixel Hollow

Inherit a tumbledown farm at the bottom of a quiet valley. Plant forty kinds of crops, befriend twelve neighbours and one very busy chicken, and watch the hollow change with every season. Scroll down and the day goes by.

Wishlist the game Play the seed game

What you'll do in the hollow

  • Grow forty crops

    From turnips to moonmelons. Each one has its own season, quirks and favourite neighbour.

  • Raise a flock

    Chickens with opinions. Feed them, name them, and follow them when they wander off at dusk.

  • No fail states

    Crops never die from neglect, they just wait. Play for ten minutes or ten hours.

  • Fix up the farm

    Patch the roof, paint the door, build a porch big enough for the whole valley.

Collect the seeds

You have 30 seconds. Catch falling seeds with the farmer; the ones you miss sprout where they land. Use ← → or A / D, the arrow buttons, or tap either side of the field.
