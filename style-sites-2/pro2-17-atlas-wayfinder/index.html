  1. Day 1 · Anchorage 1

    Saltmarrow

    The voyage starts at the salt quay, where the harbour is still raked by hand. Board at four, sail on the evening ebb.

    0 nmHarbour quay

  2. Days 2–3 · Anchorage 2

    Quill Island

    A tall, narrow island of seabird cliffs. Land by tender at the north cove and walk the ridge to the old signal hut.

    26 nmSand, good holding

  3. Day 4 · Anchorage 3

    Brannock Reef

    Barely an island at all: a low cay inside a ring of reef. Swim at slack water; the ship stays outside the reef.

    51 nmAnchor outside the reef

  4. Days 5–6 · Anchorage 4

    Hollow Skerry

    A rock with a sea cave right through it. At low tide you can row the tender from one side to the other.

    82 nmRock, use a trip line

  5. Days 7–9 · Anchorage 5

    Ember Cay

    The largest island, with red sand and a village of forty people. Three nights here: market day, the hot springs and the long beach.

    110 nmVillage mooring

  6. Days 10–12 · Anchorage 6

    Farlight

    The lighthouse island at the edge of the chart. Climb the 214 steps, then sail home under the light.

    148 nmLighthouse pier

From the log

Slow sailing, on purpose.

We sail in daylight and anchor by evening. Legs are short, between 20 and 38 nautical miles, so there is time ashore on every island and time to learn the ropes on board if you want to.

The ship carries twelve guests and five crew. Meals are cooked on board from what each island grows or catches. There is no wifi past Quill Island.
