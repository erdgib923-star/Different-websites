Concept · 2027 · one of twelve

Type One

A front-engined grand tourer, its body formed by hand from aluminium sheet. Twelve will be built, one a month, at the coachworks.

Coachwork

Formed by hand, one panel at a time.

Each wing is shaped on an English wheel from a flat sheet of aluminium: 38 hours of rolling, checking against the buck and rolling again. The roof and the floor are carbon fibre, laid in a twill weave and left visible inside the cabin.

Nothing on the body is stamped. Two cars from the same year will differ by the width of a pencil line, and the coachworks keeps a drawing of every one.

In detail

Six systems

  1. 01

    Chassis

    Bonded aluminium tub

    Extruded sills, a carbon roof structure and a torsional stiffness of 41,000 Nm per degree.

  2. 02

    Body

    38 hours per wing

    1.2 mm aluminium sheet, wheeled by hand and gas-welded at the seams, then finished without filler.

  3. 03

    Engine

    6.5 L V12, front-mid

    Naturally aspirated, mounted behind the front axle line. Redline 9,200 rpm.

  4. 04

    Gearbox

    8-speed transaxle

    Dual-clutch, mounted at the rear for balance, with a mechanical limited-slip differential.

  5. 05

    Brakes

    410 / 390 mm

    Carbon-ceramic discs, six-piston calipers front and four-piston rear, painted signal red.

  6. 06

    Cabin

    Two seats, no screens

    Wool upholstery, a machined aluminium switch bank and analogue dials lit from behind.
