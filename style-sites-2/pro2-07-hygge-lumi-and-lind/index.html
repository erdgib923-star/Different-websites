A day at home

From first coffee to the last candle.

21:00

Mossgrön mug · $28 Linnehav cushion · $64 Glimta lamp · $118 Kvällsljus candles · $36

  1. 07:30

    Coffee by the window

    Cold floor, warm mug. The first light is blue and quiet; the throw is still folded on the sofa arm.

  2. 13:00

    Light through linen

    Midday sun slides across the wall. A linen cushion softens it all and asks for a short nap.

  3. 17:30

    The blue hour

    The window turns amber, then blue. One lamp goes on and the room shrinks to the size of a conversation.

  4. 21:00

    Candles, then quiet

    Two candles on the side table, the throw unfolded at last. The most Scandinavian hour of the day.
