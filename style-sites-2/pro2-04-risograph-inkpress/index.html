How riso works

Like screen printing, but faster and messier.

  1. 1. Burn a master

    The machine burns your artwork as tiny holes into a thin stencil, one master for each ink.

  2. 2. One drum per ink

    Ink pushes through the stencil from a spinning drum. Swap the drum, swap the colour.

  3. 3. Stack the passes

    Each sheet goes through once per ink. The paper never lands in exactly the same spot, and that is the charm.

The inks

Three drums. Seven colours.

Riso inks are translucent, so where two overlap you get a third colour for free. These are our three house inks and the mixes they make.

  • Flamingo

    Fluorescent pink. Glows under daylight, unforgiving on skin tones.

  • Federal

    Deep blue. Our workhorse for type and line art.

  • Sunflower

    Warm yellow. Best as a base pass under the other two.
