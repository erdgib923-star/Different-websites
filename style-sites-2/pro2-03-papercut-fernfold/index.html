The four walks

One valley, four ways to see it.

Scroll on and the valley changes with you: fresh green, deep summer, copper, then snow. Prices are in US dollars per walker and, like the company, made up.

How a walk works

Slow on purpose.

  1. 1

    Meet at the old mill

    Park by the water wheel at 9:00. Tea is on, boots are checked, and everyone gets the day's folded paper map.

  2. 2

    Walk at the slowest pace

    Eight walkers at most. We stop whenever someone spots something, which is often the point of the walk.

  3. 3

    Keep the map

    Your map is stamped with the date, the season and anything we found. Many walkers bring theirs back next season.
