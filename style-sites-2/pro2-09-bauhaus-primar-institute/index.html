Foundation Exhibition · 14 Nov – 21 Dec 2026

Form follows play.

Forty first-year students, one semester, three primary colours and the three basic shapes. The Primar Institute opens its foundation studios to the public.

Circle Square Triangle

The exhibition

Six studies in colour and weight.

Each study is generated here from the same rules the students worked to: a 6 × 6 grid, three primaries plus black, and no more than seven shapes.

Composition studio

Your turn.

Add shapes, drag them into place, then export an SVG. Keyboard: Tab to a shape, arrows move it (Shift for bigger steps), R rotates, + and − resize, Delete removes.

Foundation year

Three workshops, one year.

01

Colour

Mixing, contrast and weight. Students paint the same square forty ways before they are allowed a second shape.

02

Form

Paper, wood and wire. Circle, square and triangle become chair, lamp and shelf by the end of term.

03

Type

Letters built from the same three shapes, then set into posters for this exhibition.
