For savers aged 6 to 12

Little savings, big squish.

Mochi Money turns pocket money into clay savings jars kids can actually see. Coins drop in with a soft plop, goals puff up as they fill, and grown-ups approve every cent.

Try the jar simulator For grown-ups

How it works

Three squishy steps.

  1. Pick a goal

    A kite, an art kit, a bike. Each goal becomes its own clay jar with a picture and a price.

  2. Drop the coins

    Pocket money and chore rewards land in the jar you choose. Every coin makes a little plop.

  3. Watch it puff up

    The jar swells as it fills. When it's full, a grown-up taps approve and the goal is yours.

For grown-ups

Soft on the outside, strict on the inside.

Kids get a toy-box of jars. You get the controls: approvals, limits and a clear history of every coin.

  • Approve every move

    Nothing leaves a jar without a grown-up's tap on their own phone.

  • Chore rewards

    Set a price for helping out. Tick it off and the coins appear.

  • No ads, no shop

    Mochi Money never sells anything to kids. There is nothing to buy inside.

  • Gentle lessons

    Two-minute stories about waiting, choosing and sharing, read aloud.
