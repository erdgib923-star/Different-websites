夕暮れの茶

A tea atelier · Open at dusk

Tea for the hour before evening.

Eight seats, one long cedar counter, and the teas of the season, poured slowly as the light goes amber. Nothing here is perfect. That is the point.

茶

Our approach

一

We buy small lots from four growers and roast our hōjicha in a cast-iron pan, a few hundred grams at a time.

二

The bowls are second-hand, mended with lacquer where they cracked. A seam of repair is a part of the bowl's story.

三

Each tea changes with the season. When a tin is empty, it is gone until next year.

The pour

Four slow steps.

80 °C
  1. 一

    Warm the bowl

    Hot water swirls once around the bowl and is poured away. Cold ceramic steals the tea's sweetness.

  2. 二

    Measure the leaf

    Five grams, weighed by eye. The leaf is shown to you before it meets the water.

  3. 三

    Pour at eighty degrees

    Boiled water rests in a cooling bowl until it falls to around 80 °C, then meets the leaf.

  4. 四

    Wait one breath

    Sixty seconds, no more. We pour the last drops too; they are the richest.
