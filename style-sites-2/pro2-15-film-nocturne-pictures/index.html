Synopsis · 19 minutes

One corridor. Eleven doors. One of them is open.

The night porter of a shuttered hotel walks the third-floor corridor at two every morning. Tonight the door at the far end, the one that has been locked for nine years, is standing open. Light is spilling onto the boards.

Over one long walk, told almost entirely in a single moving shot, she passes the rooms of the people she used to know. Each door holds a sound, a smell, a voice through the wood. None of them hold the answer. That is behind the last door.

The film is about the distance between deciding to go and arriving.
