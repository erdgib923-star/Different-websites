Hear the still water

Halcyon One are over-ear headphones built around one idea: the quiet before the music should be as good as the music.

Pre-order, $349 How the quiet works

Three layers of quiet

  1. The shell

    A dense, two-layer ear cup seals around your ear and stops the low hum of trains and air vents before any electronics are needed.

  2. Six listening mics

    Three mics on each cup hear the noise that gets through and cancel it in under a millisecond. The noise you cannot hear is the point.

  3. The amber driver

    A 40 mm driver tuned to sound calm at low volume, so you never need to turn it up to drown anything out.
