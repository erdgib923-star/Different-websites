        Questions

        Can I add a fourth lane?

        No, and that is on purpose. If something needs its own stage, make it a separate board.

        Can I import from another tool?

        Yes. Upload a CSV with a title, owner and status column, and Taskferry maps statuses to lanes.

        What happens when a trial ends?

        Your boards drop to the Solo plan. Nothing is deleted; boards over the limit become read-only.
