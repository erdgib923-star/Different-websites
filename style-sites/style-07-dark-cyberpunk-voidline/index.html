The station

Port Calloway was built to refuel ore haulers. When the last crew left, the docking AI kept the lights on and kept the doors locked. Your crew has one cycle to get in, crack the vault and get out before the port notices.

Pillar · Plan

Every run starts on paper

Scout the port from a drone, pin routes and timings on a shared blueprint, then commit. The plan is the game until the first alarm.

Pillar · Improvise

The station rearranges itself

Corridors reseal, airlocks cycle and gravity drops out between runs. No two layouts are the same twice.

Pillar · Escape

Getting out is the hard part

Loot is heavy. Carry too much and your crew moves slower in zero-g. Every extraction is a negotiation.
