House rules

  1. Whoever pitches, makes. The person in the first meeting is the person who sets the type.

  2. Fixed prices. We quote the whole job up front. No hourly drip.

  3. Two rounds. Two rounds of changes, then we print. A third costs a coffee for the room.

  4. Source files are yours. Every font, file and grid ships with the job.
