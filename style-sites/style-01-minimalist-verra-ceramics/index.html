Stoneware by Verra Ceramics

Two potters, one wheel each, one kiln. Everything here is thrown, trimmed and glazed in a single small studio, then fired together once a month.

See this month’s pieces

The kiln opens once a month

We load the kiln over three weeks and fire it for fourteen hours. When it cools, everything that came out whole goes on sale that weekend.

Next opening: the first Saturday of each month.

One clay, two glazes

An iron-flecked stoneware that turns warm brown where it is left bare. We dip it in either a chalk-white slip or a dark ash glaze. Keeping to two means every piece belongs with every other.
