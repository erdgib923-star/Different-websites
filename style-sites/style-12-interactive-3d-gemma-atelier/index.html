Fifty-seven facets, one pair of hands

A round brilliant has fifty-seven facets. Ours are cut on a wheel in the workshop, one stone at a time, and checked under a loupe after every pass. A single stone can take two days.

Three stones, chosen slowly

We work only with emerald, sapphire and ruby, so we can know them well. Each stone comes with a card that records where it was mined, who cut it and how long it took.

  • Ruby, the deep red of pomegranate seeds.
