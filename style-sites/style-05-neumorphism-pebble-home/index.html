Why it feels different

Pressed means on

Every control sinks into the surface when it is active and rises when it is not, so you can read the room at a glance. A coloured dot and a word back up the shape.

Works with your hands, keys or voice

Drag the dial, tap the buttons, or use the arrow keys. Every switch has a name a screen reader can announce.
