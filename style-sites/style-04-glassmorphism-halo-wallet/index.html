Three things it does well

Every account, one view

Link current accounts, cards and savings from most banks. Nimbra sorts each payment into a category you can rename, and shows the month as one ring instead of a spreadsheet.

Round-ups that add up

Each card payment is rounded up to the next dollar and the change moves to savings. You set the multiplier.

Bills without surprises

Nimbra spots repeating payments and tells you three days before each one, with your balance after it lands.

Questions

Can Nimbra move money without asking me?

Only round-ups, and only into your own savings goal. Every other transfer needs you to confirm it in the app.

What happens if I delete my account?

Your linked-bank access is revoked straight away and your data is deleted within 30 days.

Is my money held by Nimbra?

No. It stays in your own bank accounts. Nimbra only reads balances and moves round-ups between your accounts.
