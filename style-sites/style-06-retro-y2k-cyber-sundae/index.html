Cyber Sundae

A music and zine collective. Once a month we fill a room with speakers, play a whole record front to back, then photocopy what everyone said about it.

House rules

  • No talking while the record plays. Talk all you like after.
  • Phones face down. The zine is the photo.
  • Everyone writes one line on the wall. That line might end up in print.

The zine shelf
