In the atelier

  1. Spun near home

    Wool and cashmere come from three mills within a day’s drive of the atelier.

  2. Woven slowly

    The twills run at half speed on old shuttle looms, which keeps the cloth soft and dense.

  3. Finished by hand

    Buttonholes, hems and linings are hand-sewn and every coat is numbered.
