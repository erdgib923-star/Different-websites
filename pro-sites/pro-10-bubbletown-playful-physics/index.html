How a bubble is made

  1. Sketch. Every toy starts as one wobbly pencil line.
  2. Colour. We pick one of six pastels, all equally bright, so none of them shouts.
  3. Face. Two dots, a smile and a little blush.
  4. Squish test. Each toy gets squashed and must spring back.

Free sticker sheet

One comes in every parcel. Hover a sticker to peel up its corner.

Stickers are printed on paper backing, with no plastic film.
