A slower kind of green

Mossmere sits at the head of a damp, quiet valley where the ferns grow taller than the walls. There are no screens in the cabins, no schedule you have to keep, and a tea house that is always open.

We built it for people who need two days of doing very little, done well.

Daily rituals

  • Forest bathing

    A slow, guided walk under the old beeches. No destination, plenty of stopping.

  • Cold spring

    Three minutes in the spring pool, then the cedar sauna, then tea. Repeat if you like.

  • Herbal steam

    Nettle, mint and pine from the walled garden, steeped in a copper still.

  • Night-sky walk

    No lights after ten. On clear nights we walk to the ridge with blankets and a flask.
