How it works

Three steps, about a minute.

  1. 01

    Upload your mix

    WAV or AIFF up to 32-bit float. Leave a few decibels of headroom and we take it from there.

  2. 02

    Auralis listens

    Genre, tonal balance, dynamics and stereo image are analysed, then compared with the platforms you pick.

  3. 03

    You approve

    Audition A/B at matched loudness, nudge warmth or punch, then export. Nothing ships without your ears.

Features

Everything a mastering engineer reaches for first.

  • Adaptive loudness

    Hits the target for each platform without squashing the transients that make a track feel alive.

  • Stem-aware EQ

    Detects when the kick and bass fight and carves a little room, rather than tilting the whole mix.

  • True-peak limiter

    Oversampled limiting to −1 dBTP by default, so lossy encoders don't add clipping after upload.

  • Reference matching

    Drop in a reference you love and Auralis matches its tonal curve, not its loudness.

  • Album batches

    Master a whole record with one shared loudness and tone, so track nine sits beside track two.

  • Stereo field

    Widens pads and keeps the low end mono below 120 Hz, so the club system and earbuds both agree.

Questions, answered.

Does Auralis replace a mastering engineer?

No. It gives you a strong first pass and a clear explanation of what it changed. Plenty of engineers use it as a second opinion.

What happens to the tracks I upload?

In this fictional product, uploads are used only to make your master and are deleted after 30 days. This is a sample policy for a demo site.

Which loudness targets are supported?

Presets cover common streaming targets from −16 to −8 LUFS integrated, plus a custom value with your own true-peak ceiling.

Can I cancel any time?

Yes. Monthly plans stop at the end of the billing period, and yearly plans can be refunded pro rata in the first 30 days (sample terms).
