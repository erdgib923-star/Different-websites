Buildings that start at the ground.

We are a practice of fourteen architects. We design housing, civic buildings and workplaces from the plinth up, and we give old structures a second working life.

Measured twice, built once

Every project starts with a survey drawn by hand: what the ground is doing, where the light comes from and what the neighbours need. The drawings that follow are the contract we keep with the site.
